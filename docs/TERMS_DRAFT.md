## Current demo service

Card Club Arcade provides a collection of card games for entertainment. Solo bot matches and pass-and-play are local. Room-code multiplayer requires internet access and depends on an external signaling/connection service. The service may be unavailable, and this build does not promise matchmaking, friend presence, voice calls, or account sync.

The current player tag, invite-name list, local history, and play-coin balance are stored in the browser on the current device. They are not globally unique accounts. Play coins are fictional demo points with no cash value, cannot be purchased, transferred, withdrawn, or redeemed, and have no effect outside the game. The current app has no real-money wagers or prizes.
