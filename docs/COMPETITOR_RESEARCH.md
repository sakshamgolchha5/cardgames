# Card-game product reference review

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-24. Listings and screenshot carousels were opened in Google Play in a desktop browser. Store details can change by region and date. Findings below are direct listing observations; recommendations are design inference. No third-party artwork, logos, layout source, or names are reused in Card Club Arcade.

## Recommendations for this project

1. The 15-game catalogue needs original, consistent marks so cards are scannable without relying on platform emoji rendering. Keep the game name, player range and supported modes visible beside each mark.
2. Keep the Games library as the first play surface, with a clear Back to Games control from secondary screens. Preserve the deeper per-game setup and tutorial for players who want them; Friends stays in the compact top navigation dropdown.
3. Table usability outranks decorative scenery: hands, turns, legal action, scores and the current trick or pile should read clearly at the intended phone orientation. Existing game tables have not been redesigned in this catalogue pass.
4. Local match records and rule help are useful offline. Never display fake online counts, online friends, global rankings, persistent identity or cloud backup without the service that powers them.
5. Keep virtual coins as non-transferable cosmetic points, and keep ads, cash-out, paid stakes and gambling mechanics out of the product.
6. Provide an explicit card-size option and reduced-motion setting. Verify actual contrast, touch size and screen safe areas on target Android devices before a store release.

## Limits of this review

This is a product-pattern review of three official Play Store listings and their visible screenshots, not a full hands-on playtest of the installed apps. The Play Store pages expose listing text, declared data safety and promotional screenshots; they do not establish each app's runtime behavior. Ratings, download counts and developer data declarations were not used as quality evidence. No competitor assets were downloaded or added to the project.
